Privacy Policy for Arrow Escape: Multiple Mod

Last Updated: August 30, 2026

com.arrow_escape_multiple_mod.myapp (referred to as "the App") is a mobile game developed by vrajraj. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

1. Information We Collect

A. Personal Information

The App allows you to play as a Guest without providing any personal data. However, if you choose to sign in using third-party services (Google, Facebook, or Email), we may collect:

  • Name / Display Name
  • Email Address
  • Profile Picture Seed/Avatar

B. Game Data

To provide a consistent experience, we store level progress, in-game currency (Coins), high scores, and unlocked items locally on your device (using a Room SQLite Database).

C. Advertising and Analytics (Third-Party Services)

The App uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you:

  • Google Play Services: For app functionality and optional cloud features.
  • AdMob (Google): We display advertisements to keep the game free. AdMob may collect your Advertising ID (AD_ID) and device information to provide relevant ads.
  • Firebase: For crash reporting and analytics to improve gameplay.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Gameplay: To save and maintain your level progress and rewards.
  • Advertisements: To display non-intrusive banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads.
  • Communication: To send gameplay reminders via system notifications (if enabled).

3. Data Storage and Security

Your game data is stored locally on your device. We do not sell or share your private data with third parties, except for the advertising partners mentioned above for the purpose of ad delivery.

4. Permissions Requested

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required for ads and cloud sync.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: For daily reminders and challenge alerts.
  • VIBRATE: For in-game haptic feedback.

5. Children’s Privacy

Our game is designed to be family-friendly. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13. If you are a parent and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.
